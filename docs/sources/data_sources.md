# Data Sources Documentation

**Project: Consumer Product-Related Injuries Among Children Ages 0–17 (2025)**

### NEISS 2025
**Source:** U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)

**File:** `neiss2025.csv`

This dataset has information about injuries related to consumer products. I will use it to find which products cause the most injuries among children, the common types of injuries, and the body parts affected.

### Product Recalls 2025
**Source:** U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)

**File:** `product_recalls_download-1790116204.csv`

This dataset has information about products that were recalled because of safety problems. I will use it to learn more about product safety risks.

### Census Population 2025
**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau

**File:** `nc-est2025-syasexn-census-population2025.csv`

This dataset has information about the U.S. population by age and sex. I will use it to calculate injury rates for children in different age groups.

### Data Organization
I saved the original datasets in `data/raw` without changing them. After cleaning the data, I will save the cleaned files in `data/processed`.
